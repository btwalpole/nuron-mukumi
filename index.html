LATEST PERFORMANCE

The National Symphony Orchestra of Ukraine

In January 2026, Nuron Mukumi gave another solidarity concert in Kyiv with the National Symphony Orchestra of Ukraine.

At the National Philharmonic Hall of Ukraine, under the baton of Volodymyr Sirenko, he performed Piano Concerto No. 5 in E flat Major by Ludwig van Beethoven. Spending eight days in the capital, Nuron also ran teaching masterclasses with young Ukrainian musicians.

The orchestra continued its work despite sleepless nights under incessant bombardment... They are countering the Russian aggressor with their own civilization.

LATEST ALBUM

Portraits of Bowen

The English late Romantic composer York Bowen composed the recorded pieces in 1938, but did not publish them until 1950. Bowen deliberately did not subject his compositions to the spirit of the times, but instead committed himself to beauty: “If modern life is ugly, then there is all the more reason why music should bring beauty into it”.

Mukumi masterfully commands the miniatures, drawn with an extremely fine brush, and shines in both technically demanding and quiet, lyrical passages: a revelation!
